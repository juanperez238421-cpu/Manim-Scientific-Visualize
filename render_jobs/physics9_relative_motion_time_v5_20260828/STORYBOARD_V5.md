# Physics 9 Relativity V5 — Storyboard

## Visual contract
- 100% 2D
- 100% English
- 1920×1080, 30 fps
- white background
- black / dark-gray linework
- amber reserved for light
- large projector-safe typography
- one dominant result at a time
- no decorative 3D

## Scene order

### 00 — One motion, different observers
A metro crosses the screen while the walker performs a real articulated gait cycle. A fixed building observer watches through a live dashed sightline.

### 01 — Inside the train
The train becomes the reference frame and remains fixed. The walker takes visible steps while:
- `t'` runs 0 → 3 s;
- `X'` runs 0 → 6 m;
- `X' = X'_0 + v't` remains visible.

### 02 — From the building
The building observer is fixed. The train moves at 20 m/s and the walker continues walking at 2 m/s relative to the train. The observer sees:
- train position 0 → 60 m;
- walker position 0 → 66 m;
- total walker speed 22 m/s.

### 03 — Same walker, two velocities
Both comparison figures continue walking rather than freezing:
- train frame: 2 m/s;
- ground frame: 22 m/s.

### 04 — Galileo / Newton
The exact same velocity arrows are reorganized into:
`v = v' + V`.
Newtonian time is introduced as universal in the classical model.

### 05 — Maxwell
A traveling electromagnetic wave propagates across the screen:
`c ≈ 3.00 × 10^8 m/s`.
Then Galilean velocity addition and Maxwell's fixed wave speed are placed into direct tension.

### 06 — Einstein 1905
Two postulates appear sequentially. The scene ends with:
`So what must happen to time?`

### 07 — Light clock, train frame
Mirror separation `H = 3.0 m`.
Light pulse goes up and down.
Students calculate:
- half-trip = 10.0 ns;
- round trip = 20.0 ns.

### 08 — Light clock, ground frame
Hypothetical train speed `0.60c`.
The cabin moves while light travels diagonally.
The exact general motion equation is reused:
`X = X0 + vt`,
`x_mirror = 0 + (0.60c)t`.

### 09 — Solve relative time
A clean 2.25–3.00–3.75 triangle is shown.
Students solve:
`(ct)^2 = H^2 + (0.60ct)^2`
and obtain:
- half-trip = 12.5 ns;
- round trip = 25.0 ns.

Then verify:
`γ = 1.25`,
`Δt = γΔt' = 25.0 ns`.

### 10 — Final synthesis
Walker:
- 2 m/s inside;
- 22 m/s outside.

Light:
- c inside;
- c outside.

Clock:
- 20 ns inside;
- 25 ns from ground.

Final statement:
`TIME IS NOT ABSOLUTE.`
