## Pedagogical continuity

The sequence deliberately uses the Circle unit as prerequisite knowledge rather than restarting it:

1. Circle area and circle regions become subtraction pieces in simple shaded areas.
2. Simple subtraction becomes decomposition with several pieces.
3. Known perimeter/area formulas are compared under a scale factor `k`.
4. The final week integrates area, perimeter, circle pieces, shaded regions and scale in contextual problems.
