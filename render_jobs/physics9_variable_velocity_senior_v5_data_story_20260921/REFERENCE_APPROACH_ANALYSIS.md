## Pedagogical approach extracted from the reference

The reference does not simply present final equations. It follows a scientific narrative:

1. **Start from a model students already know.**
   Uniform motion is introduced first using equal times, equal distances, and the straight position-time graph.

2. **Make the limitation of the simple model visible.**
   The lesson moves from the simple equation to a physical situation where the previous assumption is no longer sufficient.

3. **Measure before explaining.**
   A real/experimental situation is converted into explicit measurements and then into a data table.

4. **Construct the graph from the measurements.**
   The graph is not shown as a finished object. Points/segments are added progressively.

5. **Let the pattern reveal the law.**
   The mathematical relationship emerges after the table and graph.

6. **Use worked calculations to connect the graph to physics.**
   Numerical examples give physical meaning to slope, time intervals, and acceleration.

7. **Finish with a connected scientific workflow.**
   OBSERVE → MEASURE → MODEL → EXPLAIN.
