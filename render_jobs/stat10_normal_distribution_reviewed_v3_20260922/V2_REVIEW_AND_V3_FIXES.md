# Statistics 10 — Normal Distribution — V2 Review and V3 Corrections

## Review basis

The uploaded V2 render is 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264, 237.25 s, 14,235 frames. Review combined:
- scene-by-scene source inspection,
- distributed visual frame inspection,
- layout-zone review for titles, charts, captions and transitions,
- V3 workflow gates for every-frame blank/sparse/border scanning and distributed contact sheets.

## Main V2 findings and V3 corrections

1. **Opening**
   - V2 is clean but uses relatively modest text sizes for a projected classroom.
   - V3 increases the main title, subtitle and promise text.

2. **Eight-step roadmap**
   - V2 secondary text inside eight cards is too small at classroom distance.
   - V3 removes the small secondary lines, enlarges every card and adds one large process footer.

3. **Step 1 — Collect**
   - V2 action pills are only about 18 px and the example note is about 20 px.
   - V3 replaces them with three large action cards and a much larger example block.

4. **Step 2 — List / plot**
   - V2 axis numbers, axis label, cues and dots are small.
   - V3 enlarges number-line labels, dot markers and interpretation text and removes unnecessary simultaneous text.

5. **Step 3 — Group intervals**
   - V2 places action pills, bins, table and total on one dense frame.
   - V3 reduces simultaneous text, enlarges the frequency table, enlarges bin labels and reserves a separate bottom zone for the total.

6. **Step 4 — Histogram**
   - V2 interval labels and count labels are small.
   - V3 enlarges bars, interval labels and frequency labels and lowers the plot to prevent contact with the action row.

7. **Step 5 — Shape**
   - V2 has a readable concept but three criterion cards and histogram labels are too small.
   - V3 uses a larger left histogram and three large criterion cards, shown sequentially.

8. **Step 6 — Normal curve**
   - V2 curve concept is correct, but the histogram/curve/caption frame is visually compact.
   - V3 enlarges the chart, lowers it away from the action strip and uses a large “ideal model ≠ exact copy” conclusion.

9. **Step 7 — Center μ**
   - V2 uses several small labels around the curve.
   - V3 consolidates center information into a large Mean = Median = Mode panel plus a larger μ marker and balancing-point statement.

10. **Step 8 — Spread σ**
    - V2 combines width comparison and the empirical rule in one scene. Marker labels are about 18 px.
    - V3 splits this into two scenes: sigma-as-width first, then the 68–95–99.7 rule. Sigma marker labels are enlarged.

11. **Empirical-rule transitions**
    - V2 uses ReplacementTransform between shaded regions and labels, which can visually merge during interpolation.
    - V3 uses explicit fade-out/fade-in transitions between 68%, 95% and 99.7% regions.

12. **Glossary**
    - V2 puts six rows on each page with 18 px explanation text.
    - V3 uses four pages with three terms per page, significantly larger term and definition text.

13. **Shape classification**
    - V2 shows four histograms plus a bottom reasoning block at once; the lower region becomes crowded and can visually collide.
    - V3 splits A/B and C/D into separate frames, then shows candidate B alone with three large checks.

14. **Summary**
    - V2 route labels are about 21 px.
    - V3 enlarges all eight route cards and the shape / μ / σ synthesis.

15. **Exit**
    - V2 is already clean; V3 increases the final takeaway text for projection.

## V3 readability policy

- Main titles: approximately 50–58 pt.
- Step titles: approximately 40 pt.
- Step subtitles/body: approximately 27–31 pt.
- Action labels: approximately 27 pt.
- Histogram/bin labels: approximately 24–29 pt.
- Glossary definitions: approximately 26 pt.
- No 18 px body text remains in V3 teaching frames.
- Dense four-chart reasoning is split into separate scenes.
- Major transitions use fade isolation to reduce transient merge/overlap artifacts.
