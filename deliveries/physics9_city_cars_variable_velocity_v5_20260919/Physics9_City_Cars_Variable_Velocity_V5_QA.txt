PHYSICS 9 — CITY CARS VARIABLE VELOCITY V5
Senior classroom render and QA

Pedagogical lineage:
- direct continuation of City Cars V4 constant-velocity model
- preserves City A = 0 km, City B = 240 km
- preserves Car B = -40 km/h
- replaces Car A = constant +80 km/h with:
    0.00–0.75 h : +80 km/h
    0.75–1.25 h : 0 km/h (lunch)
    1.25–1.75 h : +60 km/h (traffic builds)
    1.75–2.00 h : 0 km/h (accident stop)
    2.00–3.00 h : +30 km/h (heavy traffic)
- new meeting event = 3.00 h, 120 km from City A
- average velocity of Car A to meeting = 40 km/h
- braking micro-example = 80 km/h to 0 in 8 s -> a ≈ -2.78 m/s²

Conceptual objective:
A more realistic description of motion requires velocity as a function of time.
Real driving includes repeated velocity changes and continuous transitions during braking/acceleration.
Acceleration describes the rate at which velocity changes.
