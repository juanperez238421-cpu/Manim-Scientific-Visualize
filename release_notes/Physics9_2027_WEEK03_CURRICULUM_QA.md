## Pedagogical and mathematical checks

Worked conversions: 125 cm = 1.25 m; 0.75 kg = 750 g; 3 min = 180 s; 72 km/h = 20 m/s; 54 km/h = 15 m/s; 90 min = 1.5 h. Deliberately incorrect statement 72 km/h × (1000 m/km) = 72000 m/s is marked as incorrect because its surviving unit is m/h. Significant digits: 2 / 2.0 / 2.00 m communicate 1/2/3 significant figures and reporting increments, 0.00450 has 3, 1.020 has 4. Rounding: 4.736 m to 3 significant figures = 4.74 m; 6.284 m to 3 significant figures = 6.28 m.

These are illustrative examples, not measurements collected from students. The Week 3 videos do not add Week 4 uncertainty lessons.
